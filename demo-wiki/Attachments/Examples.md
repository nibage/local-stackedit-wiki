[Open board](example.canvas), [open Bases table](example.base), and [open multi-page draw.io diagram](example.drawio) from these local links, or click them in the file tree.

Video and audio playback depend on browser codec support. The short WAV samples are generated test tones. The PDF is a simple example document. Other supported extensions will appear in the tree when you add them to this folder.
